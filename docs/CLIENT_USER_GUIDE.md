# Client and User Handbook

## Agency SLA and Margin Leakage Tracker

---

### 1. User Roles & Capabilities

| Role | Operational Scope | Financial Scope | SLA / Alert Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Owner / Director** | Agency-wide user & setting management | Full agency revenue, direct costs, and margins | Full resolution, threshold configuration, audit access |
| **Account / Delivery Manager** | Assigned clients and recruitment searches | View margins for assigned client accounts | View SLA clocks, request extensions, resolve alerts |
| **Recruiter** | Own assigned searches and time tracking | No financial margin or contract rate access | View personal role deadlines, log daily time |
| **Finance** | Invoicing, billable placements, adjustments | View revenue, costs, client reconciliation | Confirm invoices, audit financial adjustments |

---

### 2. Common Daily Workflows

#### A. Fast Daily Time Logging
1. Click **Fast Time Entry** in the top navigation or sidebar.
2. Select Client Account and (optional) specific Recruitment Role.
3. Enter work date, duration in hours, task type (e.g., `SOURCING`, `INTERVIEW`), and notes.
4. Click **Submit Time Entry**. Entry is saved in `SUBMITTED` state.

#### B. Reviewing & Approving Time
1. Delivery Managers navigate to **Time Approvals**.
2. Review pending entries. Select items using checkboxes.
3. Click **Approve Selected** to finalize hours into official client margin reports.
4. If invalid, click **Reject Selected** and document the audit rejection reason.

#### C. Monitoring Margin Leakage
1. Navigate to **Client Margin Board**.
2. Sort by **Lowest Margin (Risk First)**.
3. Review accounts marked with amber or red warning badges (&lt; 20% margin).
4. Click **Drilldown** on any client to view underlying labor hours by recruiter.

#### D. Managing SLA Deadline Risks
1. Navigate to **Recruitment Roles & SLA**.
2. Clocks display percentage of SLA consumed:
   - Green: &lt; 70% elapsed (On Track).
   - Amber: ≥ 70% elapsed without shortlist submitted.
   - Red: ≥ 90% elapsed.
   - Red (Breached): Contractual deadline exceeded.
3. Advance stages or click **Extend SLA Deadline** if the client approved an extension.
